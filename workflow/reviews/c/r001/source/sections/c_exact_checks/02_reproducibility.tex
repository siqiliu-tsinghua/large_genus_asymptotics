\subsection{Reproducible finite calculations}
\label{subsec:c:02}

The accompanying source bundle contains a self-contained
standard-library checker, \texttt{checks/run.py}, for
Proposition~\ref{prp:c:low-genus}. It extracts the sine, $\Phi$
and $J_0$ coefficients in rational arithmetic, generates all
vertex-labeled trees by Pr\"ufer sequences, assigns all genus
compositions and rejects unstable vertices. Each edge monomial
is integrated exactly, using the same parameter at its two ends.
A genus-decorated tree has one labeling for each vertex
permutation modulo its automorphism group; dividing the labeled
sum by the factorial of the number of vertices therefore recovers the
reciprocal automorphism weight. Announced values are compared
only after reconstruction.

The source instructions in \texttt{checks/README.md} also give
the existing Appendix~A and Section~4 calculation entrypoints
and their pinned symbolic dependencies. Appendix~A's programs
reproduce its finite coefficient and core derivations. Section~4's
supplementary bundle checks the additional reported coefficients;
their paper derivations remain omitted, and they do not enter
Theorem~\ref{thm:lambda-one}.

Exact outputs and source fingerprints distinguish calculation
evidence from the proofs. The new checker writes generated data
only under the build directory. No small-genus comparison or
finite-window fit is used to infer a convergence rate or replace
the uniform graph, kernel and remainder estimates in the main text.
