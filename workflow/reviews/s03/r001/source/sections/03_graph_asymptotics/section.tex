\section{Uniform asymptotics for stable graph sums}
\label{sec:s03}

Self-loops at a vertex of large genus contribute at leading order even
when their number is fixed and positive. We therefore sum these loops
before organizing the corrections by the complexity carried by the other
vertices. The estimates in this section justify that order of operations
for the entire stable-graph sum.

We first fix the Weil--Petersson normalization and the uniform volume
inputs, then express the boundary deformation by vertex integrals.
Subsection~\ref{subsec:s03:03} bounds the absolute graph sum using
pairings, Gaussian moments and a factorial series.
Subsection~\ref{subsec:s03:04} contracts these loops and expands the
finitely many core types. Its remainders are summable over loop counts.
All expansion orders and numbers of insertions are fixed;
the graph, its valences and its loop count are allowed to vary with genus.
